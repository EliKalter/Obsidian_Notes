>Def:
>	Let $G = (V,E)$ be a graph.
>	$G$ is said to be "planar" when it can be embedded in $\mathbb{R}^2$.
>	That is to say:
>	
>	>(1) There is an injection $\theta: V \to \mathbb{R}^2$
>	
>	>(2) For every $e \in E$ there is an arc $\gamma_e: [0,1] \to \mathbb{R}^2$ s.t. (a) $\gamma_e$ is [[An arc|an arc]] , (b) $\{ \gamma_e(0), \gamma_e(1) \} = \theta (e)$, And (c) $\forall e_1,e_2 \in E: (Im(\gamma_{e_1}) \cap Im(\gamma_{e_1})) \setminus Im(\theta) = \emptyset$
>	


>Note:
>	$\theta(e)$ is defined [[Applying a function to a subset of its domain|here]].
