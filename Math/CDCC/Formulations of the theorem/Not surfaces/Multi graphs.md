>Theorem:
	Let $G = (V,E)$ be a [[Multi graph]].
	If $G$ is [[Free of bridges graph|free of bridges]], Then there is a collection $C_I = \{C_i\}_{i \in I}$ of [[A closed walk in a graph|Closed]] [[A path in a graph|Path]] in $G$ s.t. each edge in $E$ is covered by $C_I$ exactly twice.

>Note:
>	In $C_I$, $i \ne j \cancel\implies C_i \ne C_j$. (They can be different, but they don't have to.)
