


>Def:
>	$G = (V,E,\phi)$ is a multi graph when:
>	>(1): $V$ is a set.
>	
>	>(2): $E$ is a set s.t. $E \cap V = \emptyset$
>	
>	>(3): $\phi:E \to \{ \{u,v\} \mid u,v \in V \}$

>Note:
>	Any graph $G = (V,E)$ is a multi graph as $(V, E, Id_E)$

>Def:
>	Let $G = (V,E,\phi)$ be a multi-graph, and $e \in E$. We say that $e$ is a self edge when $\lvert \phi(e) \rvert = 1$.
>	(That is when $e$ connects a node to itself)
